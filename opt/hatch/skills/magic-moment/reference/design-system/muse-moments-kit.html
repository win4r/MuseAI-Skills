rules
Native and simple, traced from the canonical mobile Figma for Muse. Flat surfaces by default — no borders, no shadows, no side strokes; floating chat-layer surfaces alone carry the product elevation. Hierarchy is font weight, size, and the text-color ramp. Color is rare and means something; the blue-indigo gradient (#0064D4→#4553E4) marks primary actions. Radii: 20 fields · 22 cards · 24 banners · round pills. Striped blocks are data-ph placeholders: every one must be replaced with real media (a real screenshot, generated media, real photos) before render — the validator rejects any card that still carries one.
